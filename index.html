Campus Hub & Connectivity

Computer Vision

AI & Document OCR

Python • OpenCV • ML

AI & OCR Document Automation

An automated image-parsing script that extracts clean, structured tabular data out of noisy scans and PDFs. Preprocessed images with OpenCV filters (grayscale, thresholding, deskewing) before feeding them into OCR models to bump up extraction accuracy.

The hardest part was handling blurry cellphone photos of printed papers without breaking the parser.

Tech Stack: Python, EasyOCR, OpenCV, Pandas

Financial Metrics & Algorithmic Pipeline

Created data ingestion pipelines that calculate rolling averages and asset volatility across historical datasets. Cleaned up raw anomalies in SQL Server and plugged the final tables into Tableau for easy visualization.
